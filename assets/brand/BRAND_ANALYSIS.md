# Lexpoint Brand Analysis

## 🎨 Brand Identity Overview

### Logo Analysis
- **Primary Logo**: "Lexpoint" with distinctive "X" symbol
- **Logo Variations**: 
  - Accent (colored) - Primary branding
  - Black & White - High contrast situations
  - White - Dark backgrounds
- **Logo Formats**: SVG (scalable) and PNG (raster) for all variations
- **Logo Types**: Full, Square, and Favicon versions

### Typography
- **Primary Font**: Poppins (Complete font family)
- **Font Weights Available**: Thin, ExtraLight, Light, Regular, Medium, SemiBold, Bold, ExtraBold, Black
- **Font Styles**: Regular and Italic for all weights
- **Usage**: Modern, clean, professional typography perfect for legal tech

### Social Media Assets
- **Platforms Covered**: Facebook, LinkedIn (Company & Profile), Twitter, YouTube
- **Format**: High-resolution PNG files
- **Purpose**: Consistent branding across all social platforms

## 🚀 Brand Personality
Based on the assets, Lexpoint appears to be:
- **Professional yet approachable** (Clean typography, modern design)
- **Innovative and tech-forward** (Contemporary logo design)
- **Trustworthy and reliable** (Consistent branding across platforms)
- **Canadian-focused** (Legal tech for Canadian market)
